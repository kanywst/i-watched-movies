summary: 'In 1966, steel worker [Joseph Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Jackson_(talent_manager)) assembles his sons [Jackie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackie_Jackson), [Tito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tito_Jackson), [Jermaine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jermaine_Jackson), [Marlon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marlon_Jackson), and [Michael](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Jackson) into the musical band [the Jackson 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Jackson_5), with Michael on lead vocals. After months of grueling rehearsals, involving [corporal punishment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporal_punishment) from Joseph, the Jackson 5 perform at gigs until [Suzanne De Passe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzanne_De_Passe) discovers them in Chicago in 1968, and they are signed with [Motown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motown) a year later. Their albums top the charts, and they sell out concerts across the US, allowing them to move from a small house in [Gary, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary,_Indiana), to a mansion in [Encino, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encino,_Los_Angeles), in 1971. Motown founder [Berry Gordy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berry_Gordy) believes Michael has more potential as a solo artist.'
