Oda Nobunaga, who sought to unify the country, was engaged in fierce battles against the Mōri, Takeda, and Uesugi armies, as well as the religious forces of Kyoto. Amidst this turmoil, Nobunaga’s vassal, Araki Murashige, staged a rebellion and vanished. Nobunaga gathered his vassals—including Akechi Mitsuhide and Hashiba Hideyoshi—and ordered them to search for Murashige, offering the position of his successor as a reward. Hideyoshi devised a plan with his younger brother Hidenaga and his military strategist Kuroda Kanbei, instructing Soro Shinzaemon—a former ninja turned entertainer—to track down Murashige. In reality, Hideyoshi intended to take advantage of this upheaval to frame Nobunaga and Mitsuhide and seize control of the realm for himself.
