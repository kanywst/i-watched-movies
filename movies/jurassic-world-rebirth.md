In 2008, InGen operates a dinosaur laboratory of genetics on Île Saint-Hubert in the Atlantic Ocean, housing transgenic and mutated dinosaurs that the company head deemed disturbing. A six-limbed Tyrannosaurus called Distortus rex escapes containment and kills the employee negligent for its escape, prompting all personnel to abandon the island.
