Ten years after the Horsemen's last performance,[[a]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Now_You_See_Me:_Now_You_Don%27t#cite_note-8) three young [illusionists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illusionists)—Charlie Gees, Bosco LeRoy, and June Rouclere—impersonate the Horsemen using [deepfakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deepfake) and [holograms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hologram) to drain a corrupt [cryptocurrency exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptocurrency_exchange) owner's [wallet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptocurrency_wallet) and share the stolen funds. They are approached by the real J. Daniel Atlas—the foreman of the Horsemen—who reveals he was sent by the Eye, a [secret society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secret_society) of magicians, via a [tarot card](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarot_card) to recruit them for a mission to steal the Heart Diamond from Veronika Vanderberg, the head of a South African diamond company formed by her late father that provides [money laundering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Money_laundering) services for criminals. Meanwhile, Veronika receives a phone call from a man who threatens to expose her crimes unless she delivers the Diamond to him.
